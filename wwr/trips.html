Los Dientes del Diablo

Los Dientes del Diablo is an exciting rafting experience designed for adventurers looking for a thrilling ride through challenging rapids. Participants will work together with their guide as they navigate the river and enjoy spectacular natural scenery.

This adventure is ideal for visitors who want an exciting experience while enjoying the beauty of the surrounding landscape.

Difficulty: Advanced

Big Mallard

Big Mallard provides an exciting combination of adventure and scenic beauty. Guests will experience a series of enjoyable rapids while traveling through a beautiful river environment.

The trip is a great choice for groups and adventurers who want an exciting day on the water with the assistance of experienced professional guides.

Difficulty: Intermediate

Half Day Adventure

Our Half Day Adventure is perfect for visitors who want to experience white water rafting without committing to a full-day excursion.

Enjoy beautiful scenery, exciting water, teamwork, and professional guidance during this shorter adventure. It is an excellent option for beginners, families, and visitors with limited time.

Difficulty: Beginner

Sunset Rapids

Sunset Rapids combines the excitement of white water rafting with the beauty of an evening river journey. Enjoy the changing colors of the landscape while navigating exciting rapids with our experienced guides.

This trip is especially suitable for groups and visitors looking for a memorable outdoor experience.

Difficulty: Intermediate
