History

Founded in 1995, our rafting company has grown from a small group of enthusiasts into one of the most trusted adventure providers. Over the years, we have guided thousands of rafters through breathtaking rivers and created unforgettable experiences.

Our experienced guides are committed to safety, teamwork, and providing memorable outdoor experiences. Whether you are trying rafting for the first time or are an experienced adventurer, our team is ready to make your trip exciting and enjoyable.
